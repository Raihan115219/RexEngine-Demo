How it works

From Listing to Live Feed

Four steps. Three of them are automatic. The one that isn't is you saying yes.

  1. 01

    Import

    Paste a listing URL and RexEngine pulls price, location, specs, photos and floor plans. Off-market? Use the manual form.

  2. 02

    Generate

    Multimodal AI writes captions, descriptions, hashtags and CTAs tuned for each platform, plus creative suggestions.

  3. 03

    Review & approve

    Preview every post, edit copy, swap images and pick a date. Nothing goes out without your approval.

  4. 04

    Publish

    Approved posts publish through official APIs, right away or on schedule, to all your connected accounts.

FAQ

Questions, Answered

Does anything get posted without my approval?

No. Every generated post lands in your approval queue first. You can edit it, change the image or time, then approve it to publish or schedule.

What if my listing isn't online yet?

Use manual entry. Fill in the structured form and upload photos for unpublished or off-market properties. Content generation works the same way.

Which social networks are supported?

Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, X and YouTube at launch, each with its own formats: posts, carousels, Reels and Shorts scripts, threads and more.

How do you connect to my accounts?

Through OAuth on each platform's official API (Meta Graph, LinkedIn, X and YouTube Data). You sign in with the platform directly; RexEngine never sees your password.

Where are my photos and creatives stored?

In secure cloud storage (AWS S3 or Cloudflare R2). Originals, floor plans and AI-generated assets stay available for editing, regenerating and publishing.
